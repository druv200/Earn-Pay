Transparent Workflow

How Earn & Pay Works

No informal guesswork or unanswered phone calls. Every step is recorded clearly for both workers and employers.

STEP 01

Create a Role Profile

Workers list their skills, preferred neighborhood/pincode, availability, and expected daily wage. Employers set up their shop or business profile.

STEP 02

Compare Clear Job Terms

Every job post requires upfront wage amount (₹), payment schedule (hourly, daily, weekly, monthly), shift hours, responsibilities, and deadline.

STEP 03

Apply & Track Progress

Workers apply in one click and see real-time status updates: Applied, Under Review, Shortlisted, Interview Scheduled, Accepted, or Rejected.

STEP 04

Direct Local Hiring

Employers review candidate summaries, schedule interviews, and fill vacancies directly. Suspicious listings can be reported anytime.

{# 5. Trust & Safety Safeguards #}
Trust, Safety & Honest Verification

How We Protect Marketplace Integrity

Earn & Pay is built on truthful disclosures. We do not claim that every employer is vetted automatically, nor do we promise guaranteed employment. Instead, we enforce concrete technical and moderation safeguards:

1. Honest Verification Badges

An employer receives a Verified Employer label only after our team documents and verifies their business details. Unverified listings are clearly labeled so workers can exercise caution.

2. Mandatory Wage Disclosure

Employers cannot publish a job without specifying a positive numeric wage, payment frequency, working hours, location, and vacancy count.

3. Suspicious Listing Reports

Any user can report listings that demand registration fees, misrepresent wages, or feel unsafe. Moderators review every report with an immutable audit trail.

{# 6. Frequently Asked Questions #}
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Earn & Pay charge workers to search or apply for jobs?

No. Searching for jobs, creating a worker profile, and submitting applications on Earn & Pay is 100% free for workers. Never pay anyone claiming to collect a registration or placement fee.

Does Earn & Pay hold wages or provide an in-app wallet?

No. During this pilot release, Earn & Pay does not hold customer funds or process wage payouts inside the app. Employers pay workers directly according to the wage amount and frequency stated in the job listing.

What does "Verified Employer" vs. "Unverified Listing" mean?

"Verified Employer" means an administrator has reviewed the employer's business details through our documented check. "Unverified Listing" means the employer has registered an account and posted a vacancy, but has not yet completed manual verification. Always follow our Safety Guidelines.

How do I know if an employer has seen my application?

Every application has a live status tracker in your Worker Dashboard showing when it moves from Applied to Under Review, Shortlisted, Interview Scheduled, Accepted, or Rejected.
