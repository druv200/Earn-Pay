Data Minimization & User Rights

Privacy Policy

Earn & Pay follows strict data minimization principles. We collect only the information needed to operate the local employment marketplace safely.

1. Information We Collect

Account Data: Full name, normalized email address, role (Worker or Employer), and a salted password hash (we never store plaintext passwords).

Worker Profile Data: Skills, experience summary, preferred job categories, preferred neighborhood/pincode, availability, expected daily wage, and optional phone number. We do not collect payment-card details or unnecessary government identity documents during registration.

Employer Profile & Job Data: Business name, business category, workplace address, pincode, contact phone, and published job terms.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to match workers with relevant local vacancies, process job applications, deliver status notifications, investigate abuse reports, and maintain security audit logs.

3. Worker Privacy Controls

Workers control whether their profile is visible to employers and whether their phone number is displayed (`show_phone_to_employers` is disabled by default). In-app notifications never expose private candidate contact details in notification previews.

4. Security & Retention

All passwords are protected with cryptographic hashing, verification and reset tokens are stored as single-use SHA-256 hashes, and database access is restricted by server-side ownership checks.

5. Account Deletion Rights

Users may request account deletion at any time from their account settings or via Help & Support. Upon deletion, personal profile records and active applications are removed or anonymized in accordance with applicable Indian data-protection requirements.
