Trust & Field Safety

Community & Job Safety Guidelines

Your safety and dignity come first. Please read these guidelines carefully before applying for work, attending interviews, or hiring candidates through Earn & Pay.

Rule #1 for All Workers

Never Pay Any Fee to Apply, Interview, or Start Work

Any employer or agent asking for a registration fee, training charge, security deposit, or advance payment is violating platform rules. Do not send money via UPI, cash, or bank transfer, and report the listing immediately.

Safety Checklist for Workers

  • Verify Job Details Before Travelling: Confirm the workplace address, pincode, working hours, and daily/monthly wage stated on the job page.
  • Meet in Public Business Premises: Attend interviews during daylight or normal business hours at the shop, restaurant, or office address listed.
  • Protect Original Identity Documents: Never hand over your original Aadhaar card, PAN card, driving license, or bank passbook to an employer to keep.
  • Share Your Schedule: Inform a family member or trusted friend of the business name, address, and hours when attending a new shift or interview.
  • Report Suspicious Behavior: Use the Report Listing button on any job page or visit Help & Support if terms do not match what was advertised.

Standards for Employers

  • Accurate Wage & Shift Terms: Honor the wage amount, payment frequency, and working hours published in your job listing.
  • Respect Candidate Privacy: Use candidate contact details solely for evaluating and coordinating employment for the posted role.
  • Prompt Status Updates: Update candidate statuses (Accepted, Rejected, or Shortlisted) so workers are not left waiting unnecessarily.
  • Safe Workplace Environment: Provide a respectful, safe working environment free from harassment or unlawful deductions.
