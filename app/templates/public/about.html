About Earn & Pay

Earn & Pay is a local employment marketplace designed to connect people seeking part-time, temporary, and daily-wage work with neighborhood shops, restaurants, logistics hubs, and small businesses in India.

Why We Built Earn & Pay

In many Indian cities, workers looking for daily-wage or shift-based employment rely on word-of-mouth referrals, shopfront notices, or informal messages that omit vital details—such as exact pay, working hours, or whether a vacancy is still open. At the same time, local business owners often need reliable staff at short notice and struggle to compare candidates fairly.

Earn & Pay bridges this gap through a straightforward, accessible web marketplace where every job post requires structured wage and schedule details upfront, and every applicant can track their hiring status transparently.

Our Product Principles

  • Upfront Wage & Schedule Transparency: No vague listings. Every job states its numeric pay rate, payment frequency, shift hours, and pincode.
  • Truthful Verification Only: We display a Verified Employer badge only when a documented verification check has been completed by our moderation team.
  • No Guaranteed Employment Claims: Hiring decisions are made by employers based on candidate fit and vacancy availability.
  • Data Minimization & Privacy: We collect only the information needed to match workers and employers, and we never sell personal data.
