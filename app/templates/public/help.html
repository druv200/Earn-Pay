How Moderation Works

  1. Submit a Report: Flag any job or profile with a specific reason (Fee demand, Misleading wage, Fake job, Harassment, Unsafe workplace).
  2. Moderator Investigation: Our administrative team reviews the listing history and reporter details in a restricted moderation queue.
  3. Recorded Outcome: Violating listings are hidden and offending accounts are suspended, with every action recorded in our immutable security audit log.
